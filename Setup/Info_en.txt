S2400 ASIO drivers - quick notes
================================

ISLA S2400 ASIO Driver
  The Isla directly as an ASIO device (Kernel Streaming), 10 inputs, 2 outputs.
  In your audio software (DAW) select the ASIO
  device "S2400 ASIO Driver". Sample rate (44.1/48 kHz), packet size and
  latency correction are set in the driver's settings window ("Hardware
  Setup" or "Control Panel" in your software).

S2400 Aggregate
  Combines several devices into ONE ASIO device:
  - an ASIO master (your audio interface) sets clock, sample rate and buffer,
  - the Isla follows the master's clock (continuous adjustment),
  - optionally a Windows output (e.g. the onboard sound card).
  Requirement: another ASIO device to act as master.
  Select "S2400 Aggregate" in your audio software and choose master, Isla
  and Windows output in its settings window.

Isla monitoring and S2400 Monitor
  In the aggregate's settings window (right column "Isla monitoring") the
  Isla can be routed directly to an output pair of the master, e.g. the
  monitors on your audio interface - digitally, without a mixer. Each channel pair of the
  Isla (Mix, Out 1/2 ... 7/8) has on/off and level.
  To make this work without audio software, the helper "S2400 Monitor" (icon in the
  notification area of the taskbar) keeps the aggregate open. When audio
  software opens one of the S2400 drivers, S2400 Monitor releases master and Isla
  automatically and takes over again afterwards.
  In your audio software set monitoring of the Isla tracks to "Off" while Isla monitoring
  is on - otherwise the Isla is heard twice.

Isla feeder and Isla mixer as programs of their own
  "S2400 Zuspieler" and "S2400 Isla-Mixer" are in the Start menu (optionally
  also on the desktop). To pin: right-click the icon -> "Pin to taskbar".
  The S2400 Monitor menu starts the same programs.

Isla feeder (program of its own, also from the S2400 Monitor menu)
  Plays samples and loops through the onboard sound card into
  line 1&2 of the Isla, to be sampled there at 26 kHz/12 bit.
  Up to 8 tracks: track 1 leads (turntable 33->45, 45->78, +1 octave: tempo
  and pitch together), further tracks follow its tempo like a record. Per
  track pitch (semitones/cents), level, mute, solo, start/end markers.
  STACK TEMPO freely adjustable (-/+ or click the number and type), four
  beat LEDs follow playback. KEEP PITCH: change tempo, pitch stays.
  The turntable works on top of the set tempo (slow the loop down first,
  then e.g. 33->45).
  Preview with its own level, loop (all tracks on one common grid, nothing
  drifts apart), 1 s pre-roll, space bar = play/stop.
  SAVE/LOAD STACK stores all tracks with their settings as .s2400stack
  in the folder you choose, together with copies of the samples used and
  the folder with the single hits.
  LOAD STACK also opens a Splice stack: in Splice Export -> Stems, then
  choose the ZIP file and the folder for the stems (each stem becomes a
  track).
  Files can also be dropped from Explorer or Splice.
  SINGLE HITS (in the editor): splits a drum loop at its hits. Click a pad
  = audition, Ctrl+click = select, SAVE SELECTION writes each selected hit
  as WAV (24 bit), as it sounds (with turntable and pitch).
  SENSITIVITY = how sharply a hit must start, THRESHOLD = how quiet it may
  be. MANUAL (e.g. piano): no detection, set markers by double-click; CLEAR
  removes all markers.
  Delete a marker: right-click it or press Del. SAVE STACK also stores the
  markers and puts the selected hits into the folder "<stack> single hits"
  next to the stack file.

Important
  - Do not use "S2400 ASIO Driver" and "S2400 Aggregate" in two programs at
    the same time - both access the Isla directly.
  - Do not additionally enable the master directly in the same
    software while it runs inside the aggregate.
  - Exclusive mode for the Windows output: while the audio engine runs, the
    device belongs to the aggregate alone (other programs are silent there).
