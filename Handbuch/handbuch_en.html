1 Overview

This package brings the ISLA S2400 into your audio software (DAW) on Windows as a true ASIO® device – with dedicated drivers that talk to the Isla directly via Kernel Streaming. On top of that come three programs for working with the Isla.

PartPurpose
ISLA S2400 ASIO DriverThe Isla on its own as an ASIO device: 10 inputs, 2 outputs.
S2400 AggregateAggregate device: another ASIO device (your audio interface) as clock source, the Isla follows its clock, plus an optional Windows output (e.g. the onboard sound card). Everything appears as one ASIO device.
S2400 MonitorSmall program in the notification area of the taskbar. Keeps the aggregate open so you can hear the Isla without any audio software running.
S2400 Isla mixerLevel, pan and routing of the Isla channels to the outputs of your audio interface.
S2400 feederPlays samples and loops (e.g. from Splice) through the onboard sound card into the Isla’s analog inputs – with turntable-style tempo, stacks and single hits cut from drum loops.
For the aggregate you need another ASIO device as master besides the Isla (your audio interface). The Isla driver on its own needs nothing else.
Windows only (64-bit). This software is a private project that I wrote for myself and am now releasing free of charge for everyone. There is no support and no warranty – see chapter 9 for details.

The screenshots in this manual show the German user interface. On an English Windows all programs appear in English; this manual uses the English names.

2 Installation

  1. Close any audio programs using one of the S2400 drivers, as well as the feeder and the Isla mixer.
  2. Run S2400_ASIO_Drivers_Setup_{{VERSION}}.exe as administrator, choose the language and accept the terms of use.
  3. Choose the components:
    • ISLA S2400 ASIO Driver – the Isla on its own as an ASIO device.
    • S2400 Aggregate – the aggregate, together with S2400 Monitor, the feeder and the Isla mixer.
  4. Tasks: Start S2400 Monitor with Windows (recommended if you want to hear the Isla without audio software) and, if you like, desktop icons for the feeder and the Isla mixer.

To update, simply install over the existing version – your settings are kept. The feeder (“S2400 Zuspieler”) and the Isla mixer (“S2400 Isla-Mixer”) are then in the Start menu; to pin them, right-click the icon → Pin to taskbar.

3 ISLA S2400 ASIO Driver – the Isla on its own

In your audio software, select “S2400 ASIO Driver” as the ASIO device. Open its settings via the software’s hardware setup or control panel button.

{{IMG isla_treiber | Settings window of the S2400 ASIO Driver}}
SettingMeaning
Sample rate44.1 or 48 kHz. Over USB the Isla always runs at 48 kHz; the driver converts to 44.1 kHz in studio quality.
Packet sizeBuffer size in samples at 48 kHz. Smaller = less latency, larger = more robust. 128 is left out on purpose: at this size the Isla delivers too few samples.
Latency correction input/outputAdded to the reported latency so recordings land exactly on the grid. Shift = steps of 10, Ctrl = steps of 100, the mouse wheel works too.

4 S2400 Aggregate – the Isla together with another ASIO device

Unlike macOS, Windows cannot combine audio devices. The aggregate does this for you: your audio interface sets clock, sample rate and buffer size, and the Isla follows with continuous clock adjustment. In your audio software select “S2400 Aggregate” and you get all inputs and outputs of both devices in one.

{{IMG aggregate_einstellungen | Settings window of the aggregate (from the audio software’s hardware setup or from the S2400 Monitor menu)}}

Left column – devices

  • Master device (clock source): any installed ASIO device except the S2400 drivers themselves. Master device settings opens that device’s own window (buffer size, sample rate). Do not additionally enable the master directly in the same software – it already uses it through “S2400 Aggregate”.
  • Windows output (optional): an additional output through Windows, e.g. the onboard sound card for the feeder. Exclusive mode (lower latency): the device then belongs to the aggregate alone.

Right column – Isla

  • The Isla’s Packet size and Safety margin (Normal 2 ms, or High 5 ms if you get dropouts).
  • Latency alignment – Align inputs: master and Isla inputs arrive in the software at the same time. Isla input correction: the Isla’s converter/USB time as measured (positive = Isla later).
  • Isla monitoring: see chapter 5.

At the top of the window a status bar shows sample rate, buffer, latency and the state of the Isla (In sync, Settling, Dropout …).

5 Isla monitoring and S2400 Monitor

With Isla monitoring you hear the Isla directly through the outputs of your audio interface – digitally, without a mixing desk. To make this work without any audio software, S2400 Monitor keeps the aggregate open. The program sits as an icon in the notification area of the taskbar.

Menu itemEffect
Mixer & matrix …opens the Isla mixer (chapter 6).
Feeder (samples into the Isla) …opens the feeder (chapter 7).
Settings …settings window of the aggregate.
Reconnectrestarts the aggregate, e.g. after switching the Isla on.
Pause (release master and Isla)releases audio interface and Isla and does not take them again until you choose Reconnect.
Start with Windowsautostart on/off.

Handing over to the audio software

When audio software opens one of the S2400 drivers, S2400 Monitor releases master and Isla automatically and takes over again afterwards. The icon then shows Handed over to audio software.

Programs that open your audio interface directly (e.g. the interface manufacturer’s own software, or a DAW using the interface’s ASIO driver instead of “S2400 Aggregate”) are not detected by S2400 Monitor. Isla monitoring then goes silent. Choose Pause in the menu first and Reconnect afterwards.
While Isla monitoring is on, set monitoring of the Isla tracks in your audio software to “Off” – otherwise you hear the Isla twice.

6 Isla mixer

The Isla mixer controls how the Isla channels arrive at the outputs of your audio interface. It works in whichever program is currently running the aggregate (audio software or S2400 Monitor) – the header shows which one.

{{IMG mixer | Isla mixer: channel strips at the top, routing to the master outputs below}}
  • Channel strips: level, pan and on/off for each Isla channel.
  • Routing to the master outputs: which Isla channel goes to which output pair – several destinations at once are possible.
  • Faders and knobs: double-click = 0 dB or centre, mouse wheel = 1 dB or 5 %, with Shift in larger steps.

If the aggregate is not running anywhere, the mixer keeps its settings for the next start.

7 Isla feeder – playing samples into the Isla

The feeder plays samples and loops through the onboard sound card into line 1&2 of the Isla, where you sample them as if from vinyl – e.g. at 26 kHz/12 bit for the classic sound. Think of it as a small turntable studio in front of the Isla.

{{IMG zuspieler_stack | Feeder with a loaded stack of several tracks}}

Tracks

  • Up to 8 tracks. Drag samples from the list on the left, drop them from Explorer or straight from Splice, or use New track / To track 1.
  • Track 1 leads: its tempo is the stack tempo, all other tracks follow it as if from vinyl.
  • Per track: PITCH (semitones/cents), LEVEL, M mute, S solo, display of BPM, factor and key.

Turntable and stack tempo

  • 33 → 45, 45 → 78, +1 oct.: tempo and pitch change together – just like a turntable. Original resets.
  • STACK TEMPO: use − / + or click the number and type. Four BEAT LEDs follow the beat.
  • KEEP PITCH: change the tempo, the pitch stays. When off, tempo behaves like a record.
  • The turntable acts on top of the set tempo: slow the loop down first, then e.g. 33 → 45.

Playback

  • Play or the space bar starts and stops. LOOP loops all tracks on a common grid – nothing drifts apart. PRE-ROLL 1 s starts playback after one second of lead-in.
  • PREVIEW (with its own level) plays a sample from the list, optionally IN TEMPO with the stack.

Editor

At the bottom you set start and end for each track (ZERO CROSSING snaps to zero crossings, PLAY REGION, RESET), see the length in the original and in the Isla, and set FINE PITCH. Wheel = zoom, Shift+wheel = scroll, double-click = everything.

Single hits from drum loops

{{IMG zuspieler_einzelsounds | Editor in SINGLE HITS mode: markers at the hits, pads below}}
  • SINGLE HITS in the editor cuts a loop at its hits.
  • SENSITIVITY: how clearly a hit has to stand out. THRESHOLD: how quiet it may be.
  • MANUAL (e.g. for piano): no detection, set markers by double-clicking. CLEAR removes all markers. To delete a marker: right-click it or press Del.
  • Click a pad = listen, Ctrl+click = select (ALL / NONE). SAVE SELECTION writes each selected sound as a WAV file – exactly as you hear it, with turntable, pitch and level. File names are consecutive numbers.

Saving and loading stacks

  • SAVE STACK saves all tracks with all settings as .s2400stack. Everything goes into the folder you choose: the stack file, copies of the samples used and a folder with the selected single hits.
  • LOAD STACK opens a saved stack – or a Splice stack: in Splice choose Export → Stems, then select the ZIP file and choose where to unpack the stems. Each stem becomes a track; tempo and key are taken over.

8 Important notes and troubleshooting

  • Not both drivers at once: do not use “S2400 ASIO Driver” and “S2400 Aggregate” in two programs at the same time – both access the Isla directly.
  • Exclusive mode for the Windows output: while the audio engine runs, the device belongs to the aggregate alone; other programs are silent there.
  • Isla switched on after the driver? Choose Reconnect in S2400 Monitor.
ProblemSolution
No Isla monitoring although the monitor shows “active”Another program has opened the audio interface directly (e.g. the interface manufacturer’s software). Close it, then Reconnect. Next time choose Pause first.
Isla heard twiceSet monitoring of the Isla tracks in your audio software to “Off”.
Clicks / dropoutsSet Safety margin in the aggregate to High 5 ms or choose a larger packet size.
Recordings not exactly on the gridAdjust the latency correction (Isla driver) or Isla input correction (aggregate). An offset of several hundred milliseconds usually comes from plugin latency in the project, not from the driver.
Feeder only arrives on one sideCheck cables and input selection on the Isla (line 1&2).

Log files

Both drivers write a log that helps with troubleshooting: %TEMP%\S2400AsioDriver.log and %TEMP%\S2400Aggregate.log.
